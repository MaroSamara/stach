FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I download Stach?

Stach is in final testing and launches on the App Store for iPhone soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you on launch day.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Stach is free for students, with no ads. We earn money through restaurant partners in Stach Deals.

Does Stach connect to my bank?

No. You never share bank logins. Stach is a tracking and calculation tool: it works out who owes what and opens your payment app, and the transfer happens there.

Do my friends need the app to split a bill?

No. You can add people without the app to any split and mark them as paid yourself. Friends with the app can join a live group with a code.

Which countries and currencies does it support?

Stach is made for students studying abroad anywhere. You can switch currency at any time, and the app is available in English, German and Arabic.

Where is my data stored?

Your budget stays on your phone. Shared Split Bill groups are synced through Google Firebase. You can delete everything from Settings at any time. Read the privacy policy for details.
