Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There's no sign-up and no password.
  • Where is my data? Your budget stays on your phone. Only Split Bill's sharing features use our server. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app: Settings > Delete Account. It removes your data from your phone and our server.
  • Does Stach move money? No. It works out who owes what and opens your payment app; the transfer happens there.
