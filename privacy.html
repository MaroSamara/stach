Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

What stays on your phone

Your budget: income, bills, spending logs, groceries, savings goals, the emergency fund, and split bills you track on your own. Your profile (name, university, payment handles) and your saved friends list. None of this leaves your phone unless you use one of the sharing features below or export a backup yourself.

What goes to our server

Some Split Bill features only work if two phones can talk, so they use Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a database run by Google on our behalf. Only when you use these features, we store:

  • Your username, the last 10 digits of your phone number and a random app ID, so friends can find you by username.
  • Friend requests: your name and phone number, filed under the phone number of the friend you're adding.
  • Live group invites: your name, the group's name and its code.
  • Live split groups: the group name, items and prices, and the names of the people in it.
  • Shared split bills: the bill's title, date and total, each person's share, who has marked their share paid, and participants' names and app IDs.

This stays until you remove it in the app or delete your account. Google processes it under its own terms as our service provider; it isn't used for advertising. The app signs in to Firebase anonymously so the server can tell phones apart. There is no email or password.

Other services

  • Apple Maps: Campus Eats sends your current location to Apple to find places nearby. "Open in Google Maps" passes only the chosen restaurant's location to Google Maps, not yours.
  • Exchange rates: switching currency asks open.er-api.com for today's rates. Only the currency code is sent.
  • Payment apps: Request buttons open Venmo, PayPal, Revolut, Telda or Messages with the amount filled in. Stach never sees or handles the payment.

Permissions

  • Location: finding nearby food, and noticing when you arrive at a grocery store you saved so your list can pop up. Store arrival is checked by iOS on your phone.
  • Contacts: read on your phone only to show the list when you tap Add friend > From Contacts. Only the people you pick are saved, on your phone. Your address book is never uploaded.
  • Camera and photos: scanning receipts. Text is read on your phone; the photo stays on your phone.
  • Face ID: the optional app lock. Handled entirely by iOS; Stach never receives your face data.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs in Stach.

Your choices

You can turn off location, contacts or camera access anytime in iOS Settings. You can export your data from Settings > Backup. Settings > Delete Account permanently deletes your data from your phone and everything listed under "What goes to our server". If the server can't be reached, nothing is deleted and you can try again.

Joining a split from the web

Friends without the app can open a split bill invite link in their browser. The page signs them in anonymously with Firebase (Google), stores the name they type and the items they tick in that split group, and remembers their name in their own browser so they don't have to type it again. Nothing else is collected, and no account is created.

Restaurant deals

When you use a Stach deal, your phone creates a one-time code. If the restaurant scans it, the place name, the discount and the amount you paid are stored with that code so your app can log the meal. The restaurant never sees your name or any other details. Unused codes are deleted when you close the deal.

Children's privacy

Stach is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.
