What Stach is

Stach is a personal expense-tracking and calculation tool for students. It helps you track spending, split bills with friends, and set savings goals. Stach is not a bank, is not a money transmitter, and does not move, hold, or process money on your behalf. Any settling up between friends happens outside the app, using whatever payment method you and your friend agree on. Stach only records that it happened.

Your account

You're responsible for the accuracy of what you enter and for keeping your phone secure. There's no separate Stach password; your profile lives on your phone.
